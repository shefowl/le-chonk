🇫🇷 **Le Stratège. Le Diplomate. Le Ministre. L’Industriel. Le Général. Le Général de l’Air. L’Amiral. Le Maréchal.**

**Et au-dessus de tous… LE CHONK. 🐈**

*An entire nation. An entire general staff. One very large cat in command.*

**Hearts of Iron IV, played by Mistral Large 4 Preview (`mistral-large-4`) through the Mistral Studio API.** One model fills every role: planner, supreme command, events, politics, industry/research, army/air/navy staffs and a field marshal per theatre.

Play autonomously or in co-op. Don't like managing your navy? Enable **L’Amiral**, give it orders, and keep the rest yourself. The in-game command desk shows plans, reports and conversations and lets you delegate individual branches.

Tools cover focuses, decisions, diplomacy, espionage, construction, research, production, trade, recruitment, templates, fronts, battle plans, air wings, naval missions, supply and HQs. The model makes the decisions — including the bad ones. Our development USSR run kept reinforcements in the Far East while the German front collapsed :)

**Video:** Italy's opening against Ethiopia: planning, focuses, industry and battle plans, without strategic orders from me. Cut and sped up 2× to fit two minutes; this isn't a complete campaign.
