Mechanics are aspects of the game which require detailed discussion. Herein is a brief about each mechanic and a link to a more detailed page.

# Politics

Mechanic: Ideology
Ver.: 1.9
Notes:
A political ideology is the sum of ideas, concepts and theories to justify political action. The game has four primary ideologies: communism, democracy, fascism and non-aligned.

Mechanic: Faction
Ver.: 1.9
Notes:
A faction is an alliance of nations that can call on each other when fighting a war. The player is never forced to join the war of other faction members.

Mechanic: National focus
Ver.: 1.17
Notes:
The national focus tree represents the decisions available to steer the country towards economic development, militarization or political action.

Mechanic: Ideas
Ver.: 1.12
Notes:
Ideas are the set of laws, political advisers, research and production companies and the military staff available to each country.

Mechanic: Government
Ver.: 1.9
Notes: The political aspects of a country.

Mechanic: Puppet
Ver.: 1.14
Notes: A puppet is a country whose politics are largely controlled by another country.

Mechanic: Diplomacy
Ver.: 1.9
Notes: Diplomacy is the interaction between countries.

Mechanic: World tension
Ver.: 1.16
Notes:
World tension is a measure of how heated the atmosphere between the countries is.

Mechanic: Civil war
Ver.: 1.8
Notes:
When a country is divided on ideological grounds and no peaceful solution is available, a civil war will begin.

Mechanic: Occupation
Ver.: 1.16
Notes: When country obtains any non-core land it will become occupied.

Mechanic: Balance of power
Ver.: 1.14
Notes: Used to simulate a rivalry for power between two political factions.

# Production

Mechanic: Trade
Ver.: 1.16
Notes: Resources can be traded between countries.

Mechanic: Production
Ver.: 1.16
Notes: Production of equipment or ships is necessary for all military units.

Mechanic: Construction
Ver.: 1.9
Notes: Constructions are fixed assets that may be built in states.

Mechanic: Equipment
Ver.: 1.8
Notes: Equipment is a necessary component of combat units.

Mechanic: Fuel
Ver.: 1.16
Notes:
Fuel is a resource needed for the operation of vehicles with combustion engines, including trucks, tanks, aircraft, and ships.

Mechanic: Military industrial organization
Ver.: 1.13
Notes:
Military industrial organizations provide bonuses to equipment that they design and produce, replacing the base game design companies.

Mechanic: International market
Ver.: 1.16
Notes: Equipment can be purchased and sold between countries.

# Research and technology

Mechanic: Research
Ver.: 1.16
Notes: Research is required to unlock technologies.

Mechanic: Infantry technology
Ver.: 1.16
Notes:
Research technologies that improve infantry weapons and equipment, and unlock and improve motorized, mechanized, and special forces infantry.

Mechanic: Support companies technology
Ver.: 1.16
Notes: Research technologies that unlock and improve all types of support battalions.

Mechanic: Armor technology
Ver.: 1.16
Notes:
Research technologies that unlock all types and models of tanks, from light to super heavy chassis, as well as their variants.

Mechanic: Artillery technology
Ver.: 1.16
Notes:
Research technologies that unlock and improve all types of artillery, including anti-tank, anti-air and rocket artillery.

Mechanic: Land doctrine
Ver.: 1.16
Notes:
Research one of the four land doctrine trees: mobile warfare, superior firepower, grand battleplan and mass assault.

Mechanic: Special forces doctrine
Ver.: 1.16
Notes:
Research specializations for your special forces: mountaineers, marines and paratroopers.

Mechanic: Naval technology
Ver.: 1.16
Notes:
Research technologies that unlock new ship types and classes, as well as submarine and improve transports for naval invasions.

Mechanic: Naval doctrine
Ver.: 1.16
Notes:
Research one of the three naval doctrine trees: fleet in being, trade interdiction and base strike.

Mechanic: Air technology
Ver.: 1.16
Notes:
Research technologies that unlock or improve all types of airframes: light, medium or heavy, as well as their jet propulsion versions.

Mechanic: Air doctrine
Ver.: 1.16
Notes:
Research one of the three air doctrine trees: strategic destruction, battlefield support and operational integrity.

Mechanic: Engineering technology
Ver.: 1.16
Notes:
Research technologies related to electronic engineering (computers, radios, cryptography), rockets and atomic research.

Mechanic: Industry technology
Ver.: 1.16
Notes:
Research technologies that improve production efficiency and volume of all factories, improve resources excavation, and unlock and improve synthetic refineries.

Mechanic: Special project
Ver.: 1.15
Notes:
Research long-term projects at experimental facilities that can provide a wide array of benefits and unlock an assortment of dangerous capabilities.

Mechanic: Scientist
Ver.: 1.16
Notes:
Recruit and train scientists to help research technologies and pursue special projects.

# Military and warfare

Mechanic: Warfare
Ver.: 1.8
Notes: Fight your enemy on land, in the air and at sea.

Mechanic: Peace conference
Ver.: 1.16
Notes: Dictate peace terms with defeated enemies.

Mechanic: Land units
Ver.: 1.9
Notes:
All army components need to be designed, produced, deployed and supplied to conduct land warfare.

Mechanic: Land warfare
Ver.: 1.9
Notes:
Land warfare consists of assigning command groups to theaters, through which they can engage enemy armies and capture territory.

Mechanic: Division designer
Ver.: 1.9
Notes: Designing divisions allow to customize them battalion by battalion.

Mechanic: Army planner
Ver.: 1.8
Notes:
Designed divisions need to be recruited and trained to be deployed on the field.

Mechanic: Command group
Ver.: 1.8
Notes:
Organization of divisions in command group(s) is necessary to assign them some specific tasks, such as exercise.

Mechanic: Commander
Ver.: 1.16
Notes: Command groups can be led by a more skillful commander, if available.

Mechanic: Battle plan
Ver.: 1.9
Notes: A battle plan enables automatic management of divisions in a specific role.

Mechanic: Combat tactics
Ver.: 1.16
Notes:
Combat tactics are determined for both sides at the beginning of a battle and after every 24 hours.

Mechanic: Ship
Ver.: 1.16
Notes:
All navies require ship design, production and a naval base to conduct naval missions.

Mechanic: Naval warfare
Ver.: 1.12
Notes:
Naval combat is mostly automated through mission orders assigned to fleets and to be carried out in particular Sea Regions.

Mechanic: Aircraft
Ver.: 1.14
Notes:
All air wings require plane design, production and an air base to conduct air missions.

Mechanic: Air warfare
Ver.: 1.9
Notes:
Air warfare consists of the deployment of air wings to Strategic Regions, where they can undertake missions.

Mechanic: Experience
Ver.: 1.16
Notes:
Experience is gained mainly during combat and training, but also some national foci give experience. It can be used to create variants and division templates.

Mechanic: Officer corps
Ver.: 1.11
Notes:
Manage advisors and theorists, choose your army, navy, and air spirits, and unlock doctrines.

Mechanic: Attrition and accidents
Ver.: 1.16
Notes: Equipment loss as a result of a sustained offensive.

Mechanic: Logistics
Ver.: 1.11
Notes: All military components require supply to function.

Mechanic: Manpower
Ver.: 1.8
Notes: Manpower is needed to recruit and strengthen divisions.

Mechanic: Nuclear bomb
Ver.: 1.16
Notes: Nuclear bombs can be used to inflict heavy damage to the opposing side.

Mechanic: Raid
Ver.: 1.16
Notes: Special military operations designed to disrupt the enemy's war effort.

# Espionage

Mechanic: Intel
Ver.: 1.16
Notes:
Intel reveals information about other countries’ military, industry, and government.

Mechanic: Intelligence agency
Ver.: 1.16
Notes: The intelligence agency is the foundation of your espionage network.

Mechanic: Operatives
Ver.: 1.16
Notes: Operatives help conduct espionage through missions and operations.

Mechanic: Missions
Ver.: 1.16
Notes:
Espionage missions are ongoing tasks that operatives can perform for intelligence or counterintelligence purposes.

Mechanic: Operations
Ver.: 1.17
Notes:
Operations are one-time tasks completed by operatives for various espionage purposes.
