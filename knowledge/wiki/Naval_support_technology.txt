The naval support research tree includes all technologies which are not direct derivatives of ship hulls. For the most part, these technologies include new or improved naval weaponry, but also includes technologies for improving troop transports used during naval invasions and a few technologies for reducing the effect of incoming critical hits and for sweeping waters of hostile mines.

With the introduction of special projects in Götterdämmerung and the accompanying Patch 1.15, all naval support technologies now also generate naval engineering breakthrough progress while being researched.
