Special Projects were introduced with the release of Götterdämmerung and the accompanying Patch 1.15. Each special project is a long-term project that is carried out in an appropriate experimental facility lead by an employed scientists. All special projects require scientific breakthroughs to start, and most special projects also have additional resource, technology, and/or special project requirements.

# Categories

There are four categories of special project research. Each category has a different experimental facility dedicated to it, its own class of scientist experts, and its own group of supporting technologies that help contribute to scientific breakthroughs.

Nuclear Research special projects are available at any Nuclear Research Facility .
They involve the development of nuclear weapons, civilian nuclear power, and nuclear power plants for military ships. They are interlinked with and earn breakthroughs from computing technologies and RADAR technologies.

Naval Engineering special projects are available at any Naval Engineering Facility .
They involve the development of new military ship designs, new modules and upgrades for submarines, and support technologies for navies. They are interlinked with and earn breakthroughs from naval technologies and naval support technologies.

Aerodynamics and Avionics special projects are available at any Aerodynamics and Avionics Facility .
They involve the development of new aircraft designs, new modules and upgrades for aircraft, early missile technology, and RADAR. They are interlinked with and earn breakthroughs from air technologies, anti-air technologies, rocket artillery technologies, rocketry technologies, radio technologies, and RADAR technologies.

Land Warfare special projects are available at any Land Warfare Facility .
They involve the development of new armored military vehicles, various new artillery designs, and advanced fortification systems. They are interlinked with and earn breakthroughs from armor technologies, motorized technologies, all artillery technologies except for rocket artillery, radio technologies, and RADAR technologies.

# Breakthroughs

Scientific breakthroughs are a necessary resource to start any new special project. Breakthroughs can be earned in one of six ways:

- Spending time researching technologies related to one or more categories of special projects produces daily progress towards scientific breakthroughs in those categories. Every day of research provides +0.12% breakthrough progress per research slot being used. Research speed and bonuses are irrelevant for this, the only thing that matters is the raw amount of time and research slots spent researching these technologies.
- Employing a scientist to perform basic research at an experimental facility will generate daily scientific breakthrough progress in the facility's category. A scientist performing basic research will provide +0.05% breakthrough progress times the scientist's skill, with some traits like Genius and Brilliant Theorist increasing this rate. Multiple scientists performing basic research do provide more daily breakthrough progress, but the effect is progressively multiplied by 0.5x for each scientist beyond the first.
- Some prototype iterations for some special projects can provide breakthrough progress in the same special project category.
- Completing certain national focuses on some countries can provide breakthrough progress in a certain category.
- Rocket site and nuclear reactor provide daily breakthrough progress in the Aerodynamics and Avionics and Nuclear Research categories, respectively. Rocket site provide +0.01% Aerodynamics and Avionics breakthrough progress per site per day. nuclear reactor , heavy water nuclear reactor , and civilian nuclear reactor , provide +0.01% Nuclear Research breakthrough progress per reactor per day.
- Capturing an enemy experimental facility while it is researching a special project will grant +10% breakthrough progress towards the breakthrough category of the associated facility. This is reduced by 60% for each special project capture beyond the first. I.e., every time a facility is captured that is researching a special project for which a facility was captured in the past, the amount of breakthrough progress gained is progressively reduced by this amount.

# Managing Special Projects
Each special project category has a number of potential special projects that can be pursued. Special projects have a breakthrough cost that must be paid to start them and go through three phases. The first, establishing phase happens every time a special project is started or restarted. The second, initial research phase only needs to happen one per special project, and if a project is stopped in the middle of this phase, progress is retained for if/when the project is restarted. The third, prototyping phase is the longest phase and involves the creation of successive prototypes that build progress until the phase has reached 100% completion. The amount of time it takes to develop each prototype depends on the special project, and the amount of progress each prototype produces both depends on the special project and is also somewhat random. When a prototype is finished, it can also produce one of many possible outcomes, some of which can add or remove progress, produce an assortment of effects, and/or prompt the player to make a decision about how to proceed with the project. When the prototyping phase is complete, the special project in question is complete.

## Researching Special Projects
When a special project is selected to be researched, it must be done so at an experimental facility matching its category that currently has a scientist employed to perform passive research. While pursuing the project, the scientist will not perform passive research, therefore, they will not provide the research speed bonuses, passive breakthrough progress, or passive experience gain that would happen when performing passive research. The scientist researching a given special project can be switched or reassigned after they have worked on the project for at least 10 days.

A Scientist researching a special project does so by developing prototypes (sometimes called iterations or prototype iterations). The amount of time it takes to develop each prototype depends on the special project. Every time a protoype is finished, progress is added to the completion of the special project. The amount is random and its variability depends on the special project. When a prototype is finished, it can also produce one of many possible outcomes, some of which can add or remove progress, produce an assortment of effects, and/or prompt the player to make a decision about how to proceed with the project. The special project is completed, once overall progress reaches 100%.

Progress on special projects can also be affected by resource shortages, supply shortages, injuries for its leading scientist, and damage to the experimental facility where the special project is being researched. For special projects with a resource cost, experimental facilities pursuing them must be provided with those resources the entire time that the special project is being researched. Resource shortages can progressively decrease special project research speed to up to 0.2x. Experimental facilities researching special projects also consume 3 supply, though this can be reduced with traits like Resourceful . Supply shortages caused by, e.g., a shortage of trains or damage to supply infrastructure, can progressively decrease special project research speed to up to 0.2x. If a facility that has less than 66% of its supply requirements met, the game will display a warning in its pane in the special projects research screen. Injured scientists reduce special project speed to 0x on special projects while they are injured, though they can still be reassigned if they have been working on the project for at least 10 days. Damaged experimental facilities also pause all progress on any special projects they are researching until the damage is repaired.

Progress on special projects can also be gained or lost through the capture of experimental facilities. When an experimental facility that is currently researching a special project is captured by an enemy, current special project progress is lost by up to -20%. When an enemy experimental facility is captured that is researching a special project that has not been completed by the player and is further along in that research than what the player has completed, the player receives 20% of the progress difference as extra progress for their own special project. If this happens more than once for a special project, this effect is progressively reduced by 60% each time it happens after the first.

## Stopping Special Projects
Special projects can be ordered to be cancelled at any time. This may be pertinent if its resource costs have become unbearable, if it no longer seems to be necessary, if the player has realized that a different special project of the same category is more important, or if the facility where it is being researched might be captured in the near future. Once ordered to be canceled, a special project will take 10 days before it will actually be stopped. During this time, the experimental facility will still consume resources based on the special project's resource cost, and it will still be vulnerable to capture by enemy forces. It also cannot be used to start a new special project until the previous one has been fully stopped.

Special projects can be resumed at any time and they save both the progress of the current prototype aswell as the overall project.

# Prototype Outcomes
During their prototyping phase, special projects go through multiple prototypes before they are completed. The outcomes of these prototypes can vary depending on the project, the category of project, and previous choices made in the special project. Each outcome has a weighted random chance of firing, though some outcomes can only fire when the project has more than some amount of progress and/or less than some (other) amount of progress.
