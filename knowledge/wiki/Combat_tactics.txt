Combat tactics provide damage, movement speed, and combat width modifiers in land battles.

# Tactic Selection [verified for 1.16]

Tactics get re-selected every 12 hours from a weighted list of all available tactics to each side's commander. Combat can have different phases, with different tactics available in each phase.

## Initiative [verified for 1.16]

The side with Initiative (shown by a golden frame around that side's commander's portrait and chevrons underneath) gets to choose its tactic second and has an increased chance to counter their enemy's tactic.

To determine Initiative, the leading generals' skill levels are compared. If either side has a higher reconnaissance value in one of their divisions (including reserves), it gets a bonus of +5 skill levels for this comparison, regardless of big the reconnaissance difference between the two sides is. The side with the higher effective skill level gets Initiative, with ties favoring the defender.

The side losing this comparison has to pick their tactic first. This allows the side with Initiative, if a countering tactic is available, to pick it in response with increased probability. Its weight gets a bonus of +35% for every point of advantage in the skill comparison. However, the side with Initiative can still choose a tactic that will be countered by the enemy, as the weights of those tactics is not reduced from having Initiative.

## Preferred Tactics [verified for 1.16]

 No Step Back
Preferred Tactics can be selected at three different levels: at the country level, at the field marshal level, and at the general level. The effects of these levels can stack with one another.

Selecting a preferred tactic comes at a one-time cost 20 cp . The Flexible Organization officer corps spirit can reduce this cost.

At the country level, preferred tactics can be selected at the officer corps. They can be changed at any time, though each change incurs the cp cost. If a country-level preferred tactic is selected, the selection weight of that combat tactic is increased by +25% for all commanders of that country.

At the field marshal and general levels, preferred tactics can be selected in the details screen of the commander once they have reached a skill level of at least 5. Once selected, the preferred tactic of a commander cannot be changed. If a general has a preferred tactic selected, the selection weight of that combat tactic is increased by +50% for battles they are commanding. If a field marshal has a preferred tactic selected, the selection weight of that combat tactic is increased by +25% for all battles commanded by either them or generals in their army group.

Certain officer corps spirits can increase this effect for certain preferred tactics, such as Static Warfare with Elastic Defense or Aggressive Reconnaissance with Infiltration Assault . This applies across all levels of preferred tactics. Some national spirits, like the one provided by RAJ_i_am_death for the British Raj, can have a similar effect.

Only certain combat tactics can be selected as preferred tactics. Basic combat tactics like Assault or Counter-Attack cannot be selected as preferred tactics. Tactics only available in certain, unique phases, such as Recapture Bridge or Evade also cannot be selected as preferred tactics.

# Effects [verified for 1.16]

Despite what the tooltips show during battle, the attacker tactic damage and defender tactic damage do not change the soft and hard attack of divisions (i.e. their number of attacks). Instead, they directly change the amount of damage that successful hits cause.

Tactics combat width affects the base combat width of a battle, but not flanking combat width.

Attacking units by default have a movement penalty of -67%. The tactics movement modifiers can offset or further increase this penalty, stacking additively.

Certain tactics can counter other tactics. When a tactic is countered, its effects (including potential negative ones) are completely negated.

# Phases [verified for 1.16]

Tactics are picked according to the current phase, with battles always starting at default. The phase may change in response to picks being made, as listed in the following sections of tactics per phase.
