Support companies technology unlocks and enhances support companies that can supplement divisions when assigned to them. It also includes technologies that unlock various trains and railway guns.

For an in-depth overview of support companies, please see the corresponding section on their dedicated page.

# Support Companies [verified for 1.16]

The technology Support Equipment is the basis for the research of all support companies. It enables the equipment of the same name, which is needed for most other support companies.

FOCUS Support Equipment: Radios, Jeeps, Pontoons & other equipment used to support troops.
1918
110 days
Enables
Support Equipment 1 aluminium 2 steel Reliability 80% production cost 4.00

## Recon [verified for 1.16]

These technologies unlock and enhance reconnaissance support companies, like Recon and Motorized Recon . These support companies focus on providing the Recon stat to their divisions, which can allow them to be more likely to select advantageous combat tactics in battle. They also provide various terrain movement bonuses and are some of the few support companies whose movement speeds can decrease the speed of their divisions.

## Military Police [verified for 1.16]

These technologies unlock and enhance Military Police companies and their variants. These support companies focus on increasing the suppression of their divisions, making them more effective at handling occupation as off-map garrisons. They also somewhat improve the recovery rate of infantry brigades, allowing them to regain organization faster.

## Signal [verified for 1.16]

These technologies unlock and enhance Signal companies and their variants. These support companies focus on increasing their divisions' Initiative, which allows them to gain planning bonus faster and be more likely to reinforce into available combat width in ongoing battles. The latter can be especially helpful when performing naval invasions or when trying to hold a defensive line with multiple divisions in the same province.

These technologies are also classified as both motorized and electronics technologies, and can therefore benefit from corresponding military industrial organizations and technology bonuses.
