The ship designer allows players to create their own ship variants. Players are able to pick hulls and outfit them with various modules, such as weapons, armor, engines, and supporting systems.

Countries which are bound by the London Naval Treaty may have limitations on the types of ships they are allowed to produce so long as they remain members of the treaty. In 1936, these are: the United Kingdom, the United States, France, Italy, and Japan.
