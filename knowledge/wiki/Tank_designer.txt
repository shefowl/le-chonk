No Step Back

Tank equipment modules are being unlocked through the armor technology tree for the base chassis, engine and armor modules. The main weapon modules are unlocked through the artillery technology.
Additional supporting or special modules become available via the common tech trees (such as radios from the engineering tech tree).

# Chassis [verified for 1.16]

The chassis is the frame of the vehicle on top of which all other modules are built.
## Tank Chassis [verified for 1.16]

There are three main types of tank chassis: light, medium, and heavy. These correspond to Light Tank , Medium Tank , and Heavy Tank tanks, respectively, as well as their armor variants, such as spaa, spg, and td, and armored support companies. New versions of these chassis can be unlocked through research. Research can also unlock some more specialized tank chassis types. Among other limitations, a chassis' type determines the largest size of turret the tank can handle, thus limiting the armaments the tank can use. This can be bypassed only by switching a design's main turret for a fixed superstructure.

Engine modules represent the type of propulsion system used to drive the tank, as well as how powerful that propulsion system is.

# Suspension [verified for 1.16]

Suspension modules represent the type of suspension system used to support the weight of the tank and its crew while making sure the tank remains stable enough while moving to use its weapons reliably. A tank design must have a suspension module selected to be valid. Bogie Suspension is the default suspension module for tanks.

## Tracked suspension [verified for 1.16]

# Turret [verified for 1.16]

Turrets are the main source of breakthrough, representing visibility from vehicle, size of crew workspace and their workload. Type of chassis determine maximus size of turret. Size of turret determines size of the weapon it can mount. Smaller weapons can be always mounted. Fixed super-structure allows mounting of larger sized guns and provides a higher reliability rate though it comes at the cost of breakthrough. Fixed super-structure cannot mount AA guns as it cannot track the target or elevate the gun far enough to hit planes.

# Weapons [verified for 1.16]

## Tank Weapons [verified for 1.16]

A tank's weapon is one of its most distinguishing features. In addition to providing most, if not all, of the firepower of the vehicle, the main gun also determines which roles the design can fill. For example, mounting an anti-air gun will prevent the tank design from being used as a "tank".

Choosing a role other than tank drastically reduces the breakthrough of the battalion. Other than that, there are only minor differences in the base stats depending on the chosen role. It is important to note that different may receive different bonuses depending on researched technologies, doctrines or leaders.

# Auxiliary Modules [verified for 1.16]

These modules can be equipped in the auxiliary, or special, module slots of a chassis.

## Tank Auxiliary Modules [verified for 1.16]

All tank chassis have four slots for special modules that can either be filled with the following modules or be left empty.
### Secondary Turrets [verified for 1.16]

Secondary turrets increase the attack power of the vehicle. The number of additional mountable turrets depend on chassis type:
- Light chassis can only mount up to 1 secondary turret.
- Medium chassis can only mount up to 2 secondary turrets.
- Heavy and super-heavy chassis can mount up to 4 secondary turrets.
- Amphibious and modern chassis cannot mount secondary turrets at all.

# Tank Roles [verified for 1.16]

In tank designer you can specify the role of a design. This allows it to be used in certain armored combat support battalions or specialized support companies instead of in ordinary armored battalions. Changing the role of a design also inherently changes some of its stats, even if nothing else about the design changes. For example, a player can have two completely identical armored vehicle designs where one is designated a medium tank and the other a medium tank destroyer , and the medium tank destroyer version of the identical design will have more hard attack and piercing just from the fact that it has been designated as such.
