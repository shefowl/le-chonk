By Blood Alone

Aircraft are equipment that can be used to equip air wings and are produced in military factories . For details on how aircraft statistics affect air wing operation, see air missions and air wing.

# Airframes
All airframes have a Reliability of 80% unless otherwise specified.

## Small Airframes
Aircraft designed with these airframes can have the following roles depending on their primary armament (the weapon selected for the leftmost armament slot):
- Fighter
- Close Air Support
- Naval Bomber

Aircraft designed using carrier airframes can take be hosted on aircraft carriers.
