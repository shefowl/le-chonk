Research is used to unlock technologies. These can serve to unlock new units, equipment or structures or to improve the performance of existing units, equipment and structures.

# Technologies [verified for 1.16]

- Land
- Infantry technology: Technologies that improve infantry weapons and equipment, unlock or improve motorized and mechanized units as well as special forces (marines, mountaineers and paratroopers).
- Artillery technology: Technologies that unlock and improve all types of artillery, including anti-tank, anti-air and rocket artillery.
- Support Companies technology: Technologies that unlock and improve all types of support battalions: Engineers, Reconnaissance, Military Police, Maintenance, Field Hospital, Logistics and Signal companies.
- Armor technology (different without No Step Back): Technologies that unlock all types and models of tanks, from light to super heavy chassis, as well as their variants: tank destroyer, self-propelled artillery and self-propelled anti-air.

- Naval
- Naval technology (different without Man the Guns): Technologies that unlock or improve all kinds of ship units: destroyers, light and heavy cruisers, battlecruisers, battleships, carriers, and submarines.
- Naval support technology: With Man the Guns , the naval support research tree includes all technologies which are not direct derivatives of ship hulls. These technologies include for the most part new or improved naval weaponry.

- Air
- Air technology (different without By Blood Alone): Technologies that unlock or improve all types of airframes: light, medium or heavy, as well as their jet propulsion versions.

- Industry
- Engineering technology: Groups all technologies related to electronic engineering (computers, radios, cryptography), rockets, and atomic research.
- Industry technology: Groups all technologies that improve production efficiency and volume of all factories, increase the excavation of resources, and unlock and improve synthetic refineries.

- Special Projects
- Special project: All technologies that are directly unlocked by completing certain special projects.

# Research slots [verified for 1.16]

Technology is unlocked by assigning a research slot to it for the required number of days.

Some countries are defined with the base numbers of research slots, while other European countries have 3 and non-European countries have 2 by default. In addition to the base slots, countries can unlock additional research slots via their national focus trees (1~3 depending on the country). For most countries, they can not complete all these focuses in a game because they belong to different branches. Some of these focuses require some conditions, like having a substantial number of factories (usually 50+), and the Commonwealth countries must additionally become fully independent to maximize their available research slots.

# Research speed [verified for 1.16]

Base research cost represents the time it takes to research a specific technology. In practice, this cost will likely vary each time due to different research modifiers being applied (for better or for worse).

Research speed modifiers stack additively to research speed (rather than subtractively to research cost). For example, 110 days base cost at 15% research speed bonus will take:

{{#tag:math|\left\lceil\frac{ 110 }{1+15\%}\right\rceil= 96 }} days.

"Days Saved" are subtracted from the modified expected number of days. Thus, "Days Saved" benefit from (or are penalized by) the same research speed modifiers.

The aggregate research bonus is calculated and updated every day. Thus changes in research speed (except limited-use bonuses) apply to all ongoing research.

Research speed cannot fall below NTechnology MIN_RESEARCH_SPEED y of the default speed.

## Limited-use bonuses
Limited-use bonuses are awarded by National Focuses. Once available, a limited-use research bonus is activated when a Research Slot is assigned to an applicable technology. Once activated, the limited-use bonus is tied to the technology even if the slot is re-assigned. However, the game does give a grace period of 3 days to switch the bonus to a different applicable technology with the same slot.

A limited-use bonus can additionally act as an ahead-of-time bonus, reducing the ahead-of-time penalty by the stated amount.

Some naval technologies and naval support technologies allow the player to spend navy exp to acquire a one-time research speed bonus for them. These bonuses apply on top of any existing one-time bonuses that would be expended alongside them.

## Equipment licensing
When licensing equipment from another country, researching that equipment gets a speed bonus of NTechnology LICENSE_PRODUCTION_TECH_BONUS y. For equipment that uses designs, such as tanks (with No Step Back ), ships (with Man the Guns ), and aircraft (with By Blood Alone ), the bonus only applies to technologies for the chassis, hulls, and airframes, not to any module technologies for components of the licensed design(s).

## Technology sharing
When a country is part of a research group, it gets a research speed bonus for each other country in the group that has already unlocked that technology, up to NTechnology MAX_TECH_SHARING_BONUS y. Tech sharing bonus functions in the same way as Limited-use bonus: once applied, the bonus is locked to the tech; changes in the country's tech sharing situation will only reflect in future research. Subjects can benefit less from technology sharing, depending on their autonomy level.

## Other sources of research bonus
Various national spirits, political advisors, and design companies can continuously improve research speed for applicable technologies while they are active.

With Arms Against Tyranny , military industrial organizations can be assigned to perform research, providing a research speed bonus at the cost of daily political power .

# Ahead-of-time penalty
All technologies have a date attached to them when they become current. Researching them earlier comes at the cost of reduced research speed. Each year ahead-of-time adds a 2 ahead-of-time (AOT) penalty. Ahead-of-time bonuses from national focuses can reduce this penalty modifier. Below is a Python script to calculate the time it will take to research a technology, given its base cost, bonuses and AOT penalty.

res_perc = 0.0
time = 0
base = 170 # Base Cost
bonus = 0.34 # Research Speed Bonus
aot = 0.47 # Ahead-of-time penalty (not years ahead)
base /= 1+bonus
while res_perc<1:
 res_perc += 1/base/(1+aot)
 aot -= 2/365
 if aot<0: aot = 0
 time += 1
print(time) # Prints total time needed
