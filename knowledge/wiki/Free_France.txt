Free France or the Free French forces is a country that is neither releasable nor formable and is instead created through an event chain which will start if Germany defeats France (allied to the United Kingdom) in a war.

Free France acts as the counterweight to the collaborators of Vichy France and is tag-wise considered to be the original France, while Vichy France uses a dynamic tag. Free France will seek to contest the Vichy regime's authority and eventually usurp it and reassert itself as the legitimate government of France.
Free France, however, starts off at a major disadvantage compared to Vichy France; while they do not have to worry about occupation costs or colonial unrest, Free France starts off with very little territory and resources and is considered to be a government-in-exile, meaning that if all other major powers in the allies capitulate, Free France will capitulate as well and Vichy France will become the legitimate government of France.

# Historical background
After the Fall of France and the Second Armistice of Compiègne, French General Charles de Gaulle rejected the armistice being negotiated by Marshal Philippe Pétain and fled to Britain. In London, he broadcast the Appeal of 18 June (Appel du 18 juin) over the BBC, urging the French to resist against the Axis powers of Germany and Italy and, by extension, the Vichy France.

While many French colonial territories rejected the appeal and reaffirmed their loyalty to Vichy, a good part of them, namely the French Equatorial Africa, the French Pacific possessions, French Guyana and French India, heeded the appeal and came to form the basis for an overseas French resistance movement led by de Gaulle. Over the course of the war, Free France would continue to gradually build up a military composed of colonial recruits, exiles from Metropolitan France and other anti-Vichy rebels and resistance members and, with significant assistance from the British, begun to contest the Vichy regime's rule over French overseas territories. Throughout the war, the Free French navy served as an auxiliary force to the United Kingdom and the other dominion navies of the Dominion of CanadaAustraliaNew ZealandSouth AfricaBritish RajBritish Empire while the Free French army engaged the Axis powers alongside the British Army in both Africa and elsewhere.

# Events
Free France, like Vichy France, is created through an event chain which will start if France is democratic democratic, allied to the United Kingdom, and capitulates when at war with Germany. If this happens, France is given the option of collaborating with the Germans (which will create Vichy France if the Germans approve), continuing the fight regardless (which will allow them to continue on as Free France) or proposing a union with Britain (which will create the United Kingdom if both sides approve).

Regardless of choices made, France will be split into both Vichy France and Free France and both sides will begin to contest each other over who is the legitimate successor to France.

# Geography
When it comes to territory, Free France is a bit unusual. Free France will by default be granted control of French Guyana and the French Pacific and Indian possessions, which grants them some territory to operate from.
Additionally, however, Free France will also gain control over all French-owned and French-occupied territory not awarded to Vichy that is not a core state of the former occupier or any belligerent nation. This means that if France, for example, conquers and occupies Italian Libya (which is not a core state of Italy while Libya itself does not exist) and then capitulates, then all of French North Africa will be handed over to Vichy France, but Free France will retain control of Libya and even potentially establish its capital in Tripoli.
This means that if France occupies territories not cored by the original owner or any other belligerent before capitulating, Free France can gain additional territory to use for resources and manpower.

# Economy
Free France will retain whatever laws and ideas France had before capitulating.

# Military
As for the Free French military, the situation is even more unpredictable than with Vichy France. While Vichy France will usually retain control of at least a portion of the metropolitan, Free France, on the other hand, may, at best, receive control of a few colonial units and, at worst, nothing at all, forcing them to rely on colonial conscription and government-in-exile mechanics to build up a functional military. This also means that Free France's military performance will be dependent on how effective the Allies are in the war and how well the Vichy government is able to stop Free France's attempts to contest and take over territory.

# Strategies and guides

## Starting Position
In this particular situation, Free France finds itself disfigured by honour, strength and lands. At a certain time if it comes into its senses, Free France will merely declare war upon Vichy France to take back what is rightfully theirs. By pressure and by focuses, depending on the players decisions. The obvious choices for the free state is to rebuilt and assist United Kingdom to defeat the Axis in the current war.

## Rearmament and Building up
In this particular scenario all has not been lost. There is a bigger infantry in the frontline, a decent chunk of the navy and possibly the air force will stay on your side. Along with the rest of the advisors, a couple of generals, admirals, chiefs of staff that would fill the blank ranks. The colonies in one hand, French Polynesia and Guyana stay loyal to the cause.
So to speak, the entirety of the French industry has gone up to the Germans, and to the Vichy collaborators. It is best to start and build on appealing to overseas territories first that has been brought upon the Vichy. Use coup or promising decisions and bonuses and research the necessary equipment. The colonies that are almost all in Vichy possession will soon fall onto Free Frances hands, by focus and by decision, and the farther from the enemy faction the safe to rebuilt and rearm. Indochina is a guaranteed first win, which it will be claimed instantly. Worry less about Japan, as nonetheless the Asian Co Prosperity sphere faction is already in war with the allies. Take the necessary focuses and make sure the focuses "Intervention In (French Colony)" is taken quickly from the farthest to the closest to the metropolitan land before Vichy France declares war upon the Allies and joins the Axis formally.

## D-Day creme de la creme
The last battle with the Germans was clearly a complacency and a mistake of our part, but the chance to restore the honour shall be redone. The naval invasion surely will go unnoticed, and prior to join Germanys war, it is better to use the navy and air force to assert total control on the Channel. At the same time, organize a few naval invasion spots, notably to land in Dunkirk and Cherbourg, and have a handful of troops at disposal. As the channel supremacy is taken care off, the forces should land in a couple of coastal tiles, and if successfully rush to occupy more lands and make way for the wave of arriving troops. Rush and sweep all the way down south until Paris is liberated once again to the French forces, and take the fight against the Germans. Should the Vichy remain neutral even in this war, take almost all the bordering lands with Vichy and annex them by event, henceforth uniting the French lands and dissolving the Vichy Government.

## Eyes upon the East
Now the control of the French lands has been taken by Free France. And obviously the next war wont be in Africa or Middle East, but rather chasing the Axis back to their capital. Certainly Germany has the most of the French land possession, and to provide a proper liberation, it is best that Germany is taken first. Stretching tactic established, naval and air supremacy, and a couple of troops lining on German front. Go for the Lowlands, apparently liberating Belgium, Luxembourg and the Netherlands, and avoid going for the river protected cores as it isnt necessary to waste manpower as the general staff command used to do in the First World War. Utilize the focus to provide a little resistance, if there is any, to ensure a proper and easier liberation on the French soil. Once it is done, rush and outmanoeuvre the German units, encircling them in the process up until reaching Berlin.
Next stop is to land the fight on Italy, there is a guaranteed resistance, but less defended in the Balkan area, and in a good reason to take Alto Adige, Veneto from the Austrian lands. Keep in mind that Hungary is in the faction too, and their capitulation comes as a second priority, maintain a border with them and pour the rest of the troops south to reach in a surprise attack in the Italian soil. Do not go for the Balkans yet, solely take the entirety of North Italy, rush to Rome and proceed to take the entirety of your troops and your fight to Naples and to Palermo until Italy capitulates. While the fight is occurring, Hungary will be less of a problem, therefore try to assist the Allies next before they liberate the Balkans. With the Peace Conference next, take and annex back lands that were lost to Vichy France and Italy, and take extras if needed.
