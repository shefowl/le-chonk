Experience, often abbreviated to XP, is gained at the national, commander, and unit level, and is mainly earned through combat. At a national level, it is used to modify land divisions, ships, and planes. At a commander level, it allows commanders to increase their skills and earn traits to enhance their performance. At a unit level, it can provide bonuses when it is high and penalties when it is low, and it can be lost when units experience losses.

# National experience [verified for 1.16]

There are three types of national experience: army exp , navy exp , and air exp . Each can be used to modify equipments, designs, and/or templates associated with their field, and can be used to unlock doctrines and officer corps spirits associated with land, sea, and air combat. At a national level, the maximum amount of experience that can be stored is capped at 500 army exp , 500 navy exp , and 500 air exp .

National experience is gained during combat and training, including from attachés, volunteer forces, and lend-leased equipment. Military chief advisors, as well as some political advisors, give a fixed amount of experience per day. Some national focuses and ideas can also give national experience.

## Army experience

 army exp is used to modify division templates, to unlock land doctrines, and to create variants of land equipment . It can sometimes be used as a resource in certain decisions. With No Step Back , it is used by the tank designer to create different armored vehicle designs and can be used to unlock Army spirits at the officer corps. With Arms Against Tyranny , it can be used to unlock Mountaineer special forces doctrines.

## Navy experience
 navy exp is used to unlock naval doctrines and can be spent to obtain single-use research bonuses for some naval technologies and naval support technologies. It can sometimes be used as a resource in certain decisions. With Man the Guns , it is used for the ship designer, otherwise it is used to create ship variants. With No Step Back , it can be used to unlock Navy spirits at the officer corps. With Arms Against Tyranny , it can be used to unlock Marine special forces doctrines.

## Air experience
 air exp is used to unlock air doctrines. It can sometimes be used as a resource in certain decisions. With By Blood Alone , it is used for the aircraft designer, otherwise it is used to create aircraft variants. With No Step Back , it also can be used to unlock Air force spirits at the officer corps. With Arms Against Tyranny , it can be used to unlock Paratrooper special forces doctrines.

# Commander experience [verified for 1.16]

(Main article: Commander#Experience)

Commanders gain experience as the forces under their command engage in combat, with generals and field marshals giving bonuses to land units and admirals giving bonuses to naval units. There are no air commanders, however, air combat may generate aces, who can boost the air wing to which they are assigned so long as they remain alive.

Air units scale the experience effects between levels according to experience progress, so that 99% progress on the 'trained' level gives the same bonuses as 0% progress on the 'seasoned' level.
Note that scout planes receive significantly lower bonuses and penalties.

Newly deployed land units normally begin with 'trained' experience, but can be deployed early with 'green' experience. Naval and air units always start on the 'fresh' and 'rookies' levels, respectively. Exercising units in the field allows them to advance up to regular experience level, at the cost of time and equipment (and fuel in case of fuel-consuming units). Higher levels can only be gained through combat.

Replacing casualties reduces the experience of a unit. This loss can be reduced with Field Hospitals and certain national focuses. Air units lose -30% less experience over friendly territory. For navies, some terrain types increase the amount of casualties, and therefore XP loss, they suffer.

Changes to the template of a land unit can produce additional experience losses beyond those incurred for any manpower reinforcements that the new template might require. Converting a division from one template to another incurs 0% experience lost if all battalions are the same between the old and new templates, but up to an additional 50% experience lost for any changed battalions.

Naval units will lose 75% of their experience when they are transferred in a peace conference.

# Medals [verified for 1.16]

 By Blood Alone
Medals can be assigned to land units using political power after they have achieved certain, notable performances on the battlefield. They bestow their recipient division with modifiers unique to each medal. In addition to effects unique to each medal, each medal also increases the experienced earned by divisional commanders attached to the division by +10%, as well as the political power cost of assigning future medals by +25%. The cost of assigning medals can be decreased through the Accomplished Heritage officer corps spirit.

When they are assigned to a division, most medals give 100 experience to that division's divisional commander.
